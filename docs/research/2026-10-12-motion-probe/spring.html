A step that is interrupted, and the speed of its things

One box is leaving a rank of four and two others are closing the room, when a second box is asked to leave. The move is a critically damped spring of twenty pieces, and a piece is 40 per mille of the step. Every picture is computed by the browser from tracks opened at a moment and paused, with no script. The numbers are the kit's own reading of the same tracks: where each kept box stands a piece before the cut, at the cut, and at the cut and each of the next four pieces of the second step; then how far it went in the piece before the cut and in the piece after it.
